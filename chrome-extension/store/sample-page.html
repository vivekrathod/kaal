How to bleed a radiator

A made-up guide for a picture of Kaal. Check your own boiler's manual before you start.

A radiator that is warm at the bottom and cold at the top has air trapped in it. Letting the air out takes a few minutes and a radiator key, and it is worth doing every autumn before the heating runs all day.

What you need

A radiator key, an old towel, and a small bowl to catch the drips.

Steps

  1. Turn the heating off and wait until the radiators are cool to the touch.
  2. Hold the towel under the valve at the top corner and turn the key a quarter turn anticlockwise.
  3. Let the air hiss out. When water follows in a steady dribble, close the valve again.
  4. Check the pressure on the boiler: between 1 and 1.5 bar when cold. Top it up if it has dropped.
Start with the radiator furthest from the boiler and work back towards it.
